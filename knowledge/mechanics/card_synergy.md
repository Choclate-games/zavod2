# Mechanic: Card Synergies & Tag Combinations

Name: Card Synergies & Tag Combinations
Category: Progression & Strategy
Description: Items and abilities possess tags (e.g. [Fire], [Physical], [Bleed], [Heavy], [Roman], [Lightning]). Equipping matching tags unlocks exponential synergy thresholds (e.g. 3 Fire items trigger 'Inferno Burst' on crit).

Player interaction:
Players plan their build path by seeking synergistic tags in the shop and level-up screens.

Feedback:
- Synergy activation badge on HUD with glowing counter.
- Special combo VFX when synergy triggers in combat.

Strengths:
- Deep strategic satisfaction.
- Encourages diverse playstyles across runs.

Weaknesses:
- Requires clear UI communication so casual players don't miss synergies.

Good combinations:
- 3-Choice Upgrade Cards.
- Equipment inventory.

Technical complexity:
Moderate. Tag aggregation system and modifier calculation pipeline.

Three.js suitability:
High (10/10).

PixiJS suitability:
High (10/10).

Retention potential:
Very High.
