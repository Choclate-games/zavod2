# Mechanic: 3-Choice Roguelite Card Upgrades

Name: 3-Choice Roguelite Card Upgrades
Category: Progression & Roguelite
Description: Triggered upon leveling up (XP bar full) or wave completion. The game pauses or slows down, and presents 3 randomly weighted upgrade cards featuring stat boosts, new active weapons, passive modifications, or elemental synergies.

Player interaction:
Player reads card descriptions and taps 1 card to equip. Options to Reroll (using currency or Rewarded Ad) or Skip.

Feedback:
- Card dealing animation with glowing rarity borders (Common: Grey, Rare: Blue, Epic: Purple, Legendary: Gold).
- Card selection audio whoosh + chime.
- Immediate stat HUD update and floating text notification over player.

Strengths:
- High dopamine choice moment.
- Infinite build variety and theorycrafting.
- Natural monetizable insertion point for Rewarded Ad (Free Reroll).

Weaknesses:
- Can stall gameplay flow if cards are too wordy.

Good combinations:
- Wave Survival.
- Auto-Shooting.
- Active Combat.

Technical complexity:
Low. Weighted random selection pool with prerequisite checking.

Three.js suitability:
High (10/10) via HTML/CSS UI overlay.

PixiJS suitability:
High (10/10).

Retention potential:
Very High.
