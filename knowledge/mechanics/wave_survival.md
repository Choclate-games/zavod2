# Mechanic: Wave Survival & Escalating Swarm

Name: Wave Survival & Escalating Swarm
Category: Gameplay Loop & Spawning
Description: Enemies spawn in timed or quota-based waves with increasing enemy counts, modified speed/health modifiers, and periodic elite mini-bosses. Between waves, players get brief breathers or shop/upgrade windows.

Player interaction:
Kiting, crowd control, prioritizing dangerous targets (e.g. ranged archers, heavy champions), and positioning.

Feedback:
- Wave start banner / war horn horn SFX.
- Wave progress bar (enemies remaining or time remaining).
- Victory fanfare and currency fountain on wave clear.

Strengths:
- Clear pacing structure with built-in tension and release cycles.
- Natural boundary for interstitial ad placements and rewarded upgrade rerolls.

Weaknesses:
- Can feel grindy without varied enemy behavior archetypes.

Good combinations:
- 3-Card Roguelike Upgrades.
- Base Building / Arena Hazards.

Technical complexity:
Low to Moderate. Spatial partitioning grid + Object pool for enemy entities.

Three.js suitability:
High (9/10).

PixiJS suitability:
High (10/10).

Retention potential:
Very High.
