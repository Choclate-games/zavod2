# Pattern: Arena Combat Loop

Pattern Name: Arena Combat Loop
Primary Genre: Action Combat / Gladiator / Brawler

Starting State:
The player enters an enclosed combat colosseum/arena with starting equipment, full health, and empty crowd favor meter.

Player Action:
- Engage incoming waves of combatants using physical strikes, directional parrying, dashes, and weapon throws.
- Maneuver around arena hazards (spike pits, fire traps, destructible pillars).

Challenge:
- Dynamic enemy archetypes (light skirmishers, shielded legionnaires, hulking brutes with warhammers).
- Increasing enemy count, stamina management, and positional pressure.

Reward:
- Arena gold and crowd favor coins dropped on enemy defeats.
- Unlockable weapons dropped on the arena floor (tridents, greatswords, nets, morningstars).
- Crowd cheers unlocking temporary hype buffs (damage surge, speed boost).

Progression:
- Between matches/waves: Shop phase to buy armor plating, upgrade weapons, reforge blade enchantments, unlock combat stances.
- Meta-progression: Unlock new gladiator classes, permanent passive traits, cosmetic skins.

Escalation:
- Wave 5: Champion mini-boss with heavy armor requiring posture breaks.
- Wave 10: Final Arena Titan with multi-phase attacks and environmental hazard activation.

Session Ending:
- Win: Triumph over all 10 arena rounds, crowning champion screen, leaderboard score submit.
- Defeat: Fatal health loss triggering Second Chance Rewarded Ad Revive or Game Over summary with earned meta-gold.

Replay Trigger:
- "Try another weapon build" or "Attempt higher difficulty colosseum with trial modifiers".
