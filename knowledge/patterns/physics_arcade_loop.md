# Pattern: Physics Arcade Loop

Pattern Name: Physics Arcade Loop
Primary Genre: Physics Destruction / Slingshot / Ragdoll Arcade

Starting State:
Physics-based structure with targets stationed inside or on top. Limited ammo / attempts.

Player Action:
- Aim trajectory angle and charge release power to launch projectiles or smash vehicles into structures.

Challenge:
- Structural integrity balance, reinforced materials (stone vs wood vs glass), wind/gravity modifiers.

Reward:
- Score multiplier based on destruction percentage and remaining ammo. 3-star rating.

Progression:
- Level progression (World 1 to 5), unlockable special projectiles (explosive, split, piercing).

Escalation:
- Complex multi-tiered floating forts with chain-reaction explosive barrels.

Session Ending:
- Level clear with star score or out of ammo failure.

Replay Trigger:
- Achieve 3 stars on all levels, global leaderboard ranking.
