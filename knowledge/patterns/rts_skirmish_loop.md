# Pattern: RTS Skirmish Loop

Pattern Name: RTS Skirmish Loop
Primary Genre: Real-Time Strategy / Squad Tactics

Starting State:
Player controls a small base, a few workers and a starting squad on a symmetric map with contested resource nodes.

Player Action:
- Expand worker count and claim resource nodes.
- Produce a unit mix answering what the opponent is fielding.
- Move squads in formation, hold chokepoints, retreat damaged units.

Challenge:
- Type-versus-armour multipliers making any single-unit army beatable.
- Opponent expanding on a timer and raiding undefended workers.
- Attention as the real resource: economy, production and combat compete for it.

Reward:
- Territory and income from held nodes.
- Tech unlocks changing what the army can do, not just its numbers.
- Destroyed enemy expansions visibly shrinking the opponent's income.

Progression:
- Within a match: three tech tiers, each adding a unit that answers a previous one.
- Meta: unlock factions, commander perks, harder AI personalities.

Escalation:
- Minute 4: first raid on the player's expansion.
- Minute 8: opponent fields a hard counter to whatever the player massed.
- Minute 12: opponent pushes with combined arms and a siege unit.

Session Ending:
- Win: enemy base destroyed, match statistics (income curve, army value over time).
- Defeat: base lost, summary highlighting where income or army composition fell behind.

Replay Trigger:
- "Try the other faction" or "Beat this AI without losing a worker".
