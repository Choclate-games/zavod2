# Pattern: Survivor Loop

Pattern Name: Survivor Loop
Primary Genre: Horde Survival / Auto-Shooter

Starting State:
Single character at map center with basic starting weapon and empty XP gauge.

Player Action:
- Move character via joystick/WASD to evade dense enemy swarms.
- Auto-attack triggers automatically targeting nearest enemies.
- Collect dropped XP gems and chest drops.

Challenge:
- Swarm volume increases exponentially every minute.
- Boss swarms appear at fixed minute markers (Minute 3, 5, 10).

Reward:
- Level up triggers 3-card upgrade selection screen.
- Evolving weapon combinations (Weapon Lv 8 + Passive Item -> Super Weapon).

Progression:
- Mid-run: Build synergized arsenal of 6 active weapons and 6 passive accessories.
- Meta-progression: Permanent shop (Move speed, Magnet range, Max HP, Rerolls).

Escalation:
- Screen-filling mob density, swarms of elites with projectile shields.

Session Ending:
- 15-minute or 20-minute survival victory or death by horde.

Replay Trigger:
- Unlocked new character with unique starting weapon, new arena biome.
