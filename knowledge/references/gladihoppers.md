# Reference Game: Gladihoppers

Game: Gladihoppers
Genre: 2D/3D Physics Gladiator Battler
Core loop: Choose arena -> Fight match using hopping and directional strikes -> Earn gold/fame -> Buy weapons and shields -> Fight tournament boss.
Core mechanic: Fast hopping movement, stance switching, weapon weight impacts, ragdoll knockbacks.
Progression: Equipment tier upgrades, stat attributes (strength, agility, stamina), career tournament mode.
Retention: Addictive quick 60-second matches, humorous physics deaths, weapon collection compulsion.
Monetization: Interstitial ads after tournament rounds, rewarded ads for bonus gold or reviving in championship.
Visual identity: Low-poly pixel hybrid 2.5D Roman arena aesthetic, distinct weapon silhouettes.
Why it works: Tactile combat where weapon strikes have physical presence rather than just stat subtraction.
What can be learned: Stance-based attacks combined with physics weight provide deep emergent combat in small file size.
What should NOT be copied: Floaty controls without input buffering; lack of meta-progression in early versions.
