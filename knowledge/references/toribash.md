# Reference Game: Toribash

Game: Toribash
Genre: Turn-based Physics Combat Sandbox
Core loop: Preview joint forces -> Step turn -> Watch physics resolution -> Adapt to opponent's momentum.
Core mechanic: Individual joint control (contract, extend, hold, relax) driving physical momentum and force transfer.
Progression: Belt ranking, player customizations.
Retention: High skill ceiling and emergent viral fight replays.
Monetization: Cosmetic items and tournament entry tickets.
Visual identity: Minimalist mannequin ragdolls in clean white/black dojo.
Why it works: Uncompromising physical simulation where every joint movement impacts center of mass.
What can be learned: Kinetic momentum transfer and center-of-mass balance are the core secrets of satisfying physics combat.
What should NOT be copied: Ultra-complex multi-joint controls unsuitable for real-time mobile browser play.
